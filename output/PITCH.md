## 1. Offer Summary

- **Who it's for**: EU Amazon FBA sellers with 50–500 SKUs who are expanding to Zalando, have received a Zalando Partner Programme image rejection, or are anticipating the format conflict between Amazon's 1:1 square requirement and Zalando's 2:3 portrait requirement. Upload raw photos → BiRefNet AI removes backgrounds (including glass, jewelry, transparent packaging, reflective surfaces) → auto-centering applies marketplace-specific margins → compliance presets output Amazon (2000×2000px, RGB 255,255,255, 5% margin) and Zalando (1500×2250px, portrait 2:3, 12% margin) files simultaneously → organized download folders by marketplace → ready to upload to Seller Central or Zalando Partner Portal in the same session.

Complex product before/after gallery (glass, jewelry, transparent packaging) is the highest-impact single trust investment available with no product changes.

**Time Delay: 8/10**
Processing speed is strong: first result visible in 60 seconds, full 200-image catalog in under 10 minutes. Gap: the buyer's emotional first win is a listing going live without rejection, not a file in a download folder. Fix applied: copy bridges from "download" to "your Zalando application is ready to submit today" — moving the perceived finish line from the tool to the marketplace outcome.

**Effort & Sacrifice: 9/10**
Effort reduction is very strong: zero configuration, zero skills, no installation, drag-upload-download in one browser session. Gap: first-time users face a "what do I do immediately after signup?" ambiguity. Fix applied: the 10-Minute Catalog Kickstart Guide bonus is deployed on the signup confirmation screen — the onboarding action is the first thing they see, not a dashboard.
