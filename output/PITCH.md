## 3. Pitch LUNGO — versione estesa (EN)

> Uso: seconda email, pagina "come funziona", pitch parlato ~60-90s, call. Stessa spina: moat prima.

**Callout + Pain**
> If you run a real catalog — dozens to hundreds of SKUs that you keep adding to — your product photos
> almost certainly don't match. They were shot on different days, at different distances, on different
> backgrounds, so sizes drift, products sit off-center, exposure jumps from one photo to the next. Next
> to a bigger brand it looks amateur. And it's not a one-time problem: every seasonal drop and every new
> SKU breaks the grid, so you re-align by hand in Canva or re-brief a Fiverr freelancer who starts from
> zero every time.

**Dream outcome**
> What you actually want is a catalog where every product photo matches every other — same size, same
> centering, same clean background, same exposure — and *stays* matched as the catalog grows. Set the
> look once; never redo it by hand again.

**Solution — Pillar 1 (il moat, si guida qui)**
> That's what Centyr does. Upload your catalog and it aligns, centers, sizes, removes the background,
> and normalizes exposure to one reproducible standard — ~200 products in about 10 minutes, versus ~20
> hours by hand (~€300 of manual work). A single product processes in about 21 seconds.
>
> The part nothing else does: the standard *persists*. Add a new product three months from now and it
> **auto-matches** the 500 already in your catalog. Change your margin, background, or size and **one
> click re-applies** the new standard to the entire catalog. A freelancer or a Canva session cleans it
> up once and it drifts again; Centyr keeps it uniform over time. That's the difference between renting
> labor every cycle and owning a standard you keep.

**Solution — Pillar 2 (supporto: multichannel)**
> If you sell on more than one channel, the same standardized catalog exports to each one's format from
> a single upload — Amazon square, Zalando portrait, eBay, Etsy, and your own store — so you never
> reformat for each platform by hand.

**Solution — Pillar 3 (supporto: done-for-you compliance)**
> And you don't have to track any of it. Each marketplace publishes its own image spec and changes it;
> Centyr keeps up with all of them so you never learn, track, or maintain a single one.

**Likelihood / prova onesta**
> On the hard products — glass, jewelry, transparent packaging, reflective surfaces — Centyr uses
> BiRefNet, a model chosen specifically for those edge cases where generic tools fail. And you don't
> have to take my word for it: send me your hardest products and I'll show you the before/after on your
> *own* catalog before you pay anything. If you subscribe and it doesn't give you a uniform, reproducible
> catalog, there's a 30-day money-back guarantee.

**Prezzo (contrasto onesto)**
> Pro is €39.99/month. One manual alignment pass on ~200 products is ~20 hours (~€300) — and it comes
> back every season and every new drop. Centyr does it in ~10 minutes and then holds the standard, so
> the saving repeats.

**CTA (uno solo)**
> Want me to run a free before/after on a sample of your real products — including your most difficult
> ones — so you can see your own catalog standardized before you decide? Just send me a handful.
