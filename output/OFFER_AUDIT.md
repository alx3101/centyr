# Centyr — Hormozi Value Equation Audit
# Offer: Catalog Passport by Centyr

---

## 1. Offer Summary

- **Who it's for**: EU Amazon FBA sellers with 50–500 SKUs who are expanding to (or being rejected by) Zalando, with no design team and an existing catalog of product photos that are already square/Amazon-formatted. Secondary: e-commerce agencies managing 5+ client catalogs.
- **What it promises**: One upload of raw product photos generates both Amazon-compliant images (2000×2000, white background, 5% margin) AND Zalando-compliant images (1500×2250, 2:3 portrait, 12% margin) in under 10 minutes — with zero Photoshop, no studio booking, no design skills.
- **How it works**: SaaS web application. Drag raw photos in → BiRefNet AI removes backgrounds → auto-centering with configurable margin → simultaneous output in all selected marketplace presets → download organized folders per platform.
- **Price**: €29–€99/month depending on volume tier (hypothesis stage). Growth tier hypothesis: €59/month or €49/month billed annually. Free trial: 25 images, no credit card.
- **ROI anchor**: €6,000 per 200-product catalog cycle at studio rates (€30/image × 200) vs. €588/year for Centyr = 10:1 ROI in month one.

---

## 2. Overall Diagnosis

**Strengths:**
- Segment precision is exceptional. The Amazon-to-Zalando expansion moment is one of the most targetable trigger events in EU e-commerce — calculable financial pain, specific rejection message, confirmed spend on inferior alternatives. Scores 34/40 in market research.
- The format conflict positioning (Amazon 1:1 square vs. Zalando 2:3 portrait) is a genuine, verifiable structural differentiator. No identified competitor resolves both specs in a single automated batch.
- ROI case is immediately obvious. The €6,000 studio alternative makes even €99/month feel trivial. The ROI is not vague — it is calculable per image and per catalog.
- Free trial design (25 images, no credit card) is the right mechanism. It functions as acquisition, trust-building, and objection-handling simultaneously.
- Messaging angles are strong at the hook level. The pain-based Zalando rejection hook, studio cost anchor, and blocked inventory angle are all specific and conversion-ready.
- Solution map is logically complete — every obstacle has a corresponding product feature.

**Critical Weaknesses:**
- Zero social proof anywhere in the offer structure. No testimonials, no before/after compliance gallery, no pass rate data, no user count. Every prospect must personally experiment before they believe the AI output is good enough. Social proof would shorten this gap for a significant portion of visitors who won't click a free trial without some external validation.
- Bonus stack is entirely informational. All five bonuses are described as PDFs, guides, and checklists — formats associated with free lead magnets, not premium deliverables. This undermines the stated total bonus value of €225 and weakens the perceived value of the entire stack.
- Three guarantee options without a committed choice. Internally this is a strategy decision; externally it produces vague messaging. No live guarantee statement currently exists on the product.
- Pricing is still a range. A €29–€99 spread signals that the offer isn't finalized, which weakens anchoring on any page that attempts to show ROI.
- Live hero copy is underperforming relative to the best angles developed in OFFER_ANGLES.md. The gap between documented strategy and live execution represents immediate, measurable conversion loss.

## 4. Top 3 Priority Fixes

**Fix 1 — Build the proof layer before any paid acquisition (Impact: Highest / Fixes Perceived Likelihood + Objections & Trust)**

The offer has no social proof and scores 5/10 on both Perceived Likelihood and Objections & Trust. Every prospect who does not already know Centyr must personally experiment before they believe the AI output is marketplace-compliant. Social proof eliminates this requirement for a significant portion of visitors — they believe because others have already verified.

Specific actions:
- Publish 6–8 before/after image pairs for complex product types (glass bottle, gold jewelry, transparent packaging, shiny metallic surfaces, dark background). Each pair shows: raw photo → Centyr output → "Submitted to Amazon / Accepted" or "Submitted to Zalando / Accepted."
- Add a processing volume counter to the homepage. Even a low real number — "4,200 marketplace images processed" — signals the product is in use and trusted. Zero counter signals zero adoption.
- Collect the first real user quote at the first opportunity. One specific sentence from a real seller ("Processed 150 SKUs in 40 minutes, all accepted on first Zalando submission — [Name], Amazon seller, IT") outperforms a full paragraph of product copy.
- If no user testimonials exist yet: publish a BiRefNet AI pass rate statistic ("X% of images processed meet marketplace compliance specifications on first submission") — a data point backed by batch testing is better than no proof.

This fix should be in place before any paid acquisition budget is spent.

---

**Fix 2 — Commit to one guarantee and make it the front-of-offer headline mechanism (Impact: High / Fixes Offer Structure + Trust)**

Three guarantee options is an internal strategy artifact. Externally it produces no guarantee at all because none of the three is published or named. The free trial (Option 3) is already the best mechanism — it eliminates all purchase risk by letting results prove themselves before any money changes hands.

Specific actions:
- Name the guarantee: "The Zero-Rejection First Batch Guarantee — Process your first 25 images free. No credit card. If the output isn't marketplace-compliant, you don't pay. Ever."
- This named guarantee becomes the primary CTA, the primary risk reversal, and the primary acquisition hook simultaneously. It belongs in the hero section, below the CTA button, and repeated near any pricing display.
- For active paid subscribers, deploy the conditional guarantee (Option 2 from OFFER.md) as a post-purchase retention layer: "If more than 2 images in any paid batch are rejected by Amazon or Zalando for compliance reasons, we reprocess the entire batch free — or refund you." Communicate this post-signup, not on the acquisition page.

---

**Fix 3 — Upgrade the bonus stack from documents to execution assets (Impact: Medium-High / Fixes Value Stack + Effort Reduction)**

Documents feel free. Tools feel valuable. When every bonus in a €225 stack is described as a "cheat sheet," "checklist," or "guide," the stated value is unconvincing — these are formats prospects associate with opt-in lead magnets. At least two bonuses should do the work for the buyer rather than inform them.

Specific actions:
- Upgrade "Catalog Launch Checklist" (€27) to a "Pre-Submission Safety Net" — a fillable Google Sheet that auto-checks each processed image against Amazon and Zalando's compliance criteria. The buyer fills in their file names, the sheet flags anything below spec. It does the audit for them rather than telling them how to audit.
- Upgrade "Seasonal Catalog Workflow Template" (€47) to a "Catalog Engine SOP Pack" — a ready-to-use Notion or Google Sheets template with pre-built naming conventions, folder structures, upload tracking, and per-platform status columns. Not a guide on how to build this — the built system, ready to copy and use.
- For the first 30–50 subscribers, consider adding a high-perceived-value bonus service: "Zalando Partner Programme Image Audit" — manual review of their first processed batch before submission by a Centyr team member. Estimated perceived value: €150+. Actual operational cost: 20 minutes per batch. This is the single highest-trust conversion accelerator available at early stage.

## 5. Quick Wins (implement immediately, no development required)

- Replace the live hero headline with the pain-based H1: "Zalando rejected your images because they're square. Your Amazon catalog is already the reshoot. Upload once — get both formats in under 10 minutes." This is a copy change with zero development dependency.
- Add "No credit card required. First 25 images free." as badge text directly below every CTA button. This removes the unstated objection (fear of being charged) before it forms.
- Name the guarantee in the primary CTA button copy. "Process Your First 25 Free — Zero-Rejection Guarantee" outperforms "Start Free Trial" because it answers the primary objection in the action itself.
- Publish one complex product before/after image above the fold on the homepage. Glass or jewelry. Raw vs. Centyr output. No text required — the image answers the highest-frequency objection before it is raised.
- Add a processing volume counter anywhere on the homepage. Any real number creates the signal that the product is in active use.
- Surface the €6,000 studio comparison explicitly at the pricing point: a two-column visual — "Photo studio for 200 products: €6,000 / 5–10 business days / inconsistent results" vs. "Centyr: €59/month / 10 minutes / guaranteed compliant output."
