# Centyr — Positioning Angles
# Offer: Catalog Passport by Centyr

---

## Base Offer

- **Who**: EU Amazon FBA sellers with 50–500 SKUs expanding to Zalando (and any e-commerce seller managing images across multiple marketplaces with incompatible format requirements)
- **What**: One upload that outputs Amazon-compliant AND Zalando-compliant product images simultaneously — AI background removal, auto-centering, exact marketplace format presets — in under 10 minutes, zero design skills required
- **Current positioning**: "AI-powered product photo processor for e-commerce" (generic; does not activate the format conflict pain or the Zalando trigger)

---

## Generated Angles

### Angle 1: Outcome-Specific

Frame the value in concrete, countable results — not features.

- **V1**: "Get 200 Amazon-ready AND 200 Zalando-ready product images from one batch upload — without touching Photoshop, booking a studio, or paying €30 per image."
- **V2**: "One raw photo in. Two marketplace-compliant files out. For every product in your catalog, simultaneously."

---

### Angle 2: Time-Based

Add a concrete time frame to the promise — makes the result feel achievable and imminent.

- **V1**: "Turn your entire product catalog into Zalando-compliant images in under 10 minutes — the same catalog that would take a studio 5 business days and €4,000."
- **V2**: "From raw photos to marketplace-ready catalog in one afternoon — so you can submit your Zalando application this week, not next quarter."

---

### Angle 3: Pain-Based

Lead with the most visceral, urgent frustration. No solution mentioned until the pain is fully named.

- **V1**: "Zalando rejected your images because they want portrait format and all your photos are square. You don't need a reshoot. You need Centyr."
- **V2**: "Every day your Amazon listings are suppressed, you're paying for ads to dead pages. The problem isn't your product. It's one inconsistent pixel in your background."

---

### Angle 4: Identity-Based

Connect the offer to who the buyer wants to become — the self-image shift, not the feature.

- **V1**: "Run your e-commerce catalog like a brand that belongs on every major EU marketplace — not a seller who's still figuring out why Zalando keeps rejecting their images."
- **V2**: "Stop improvising your image workflow every season. Build the catalog infrastructure that makes you look like a professional brand — automatically."

---

### Angle 5: Effort-Based

Emphasize the elimination of work, skill requirements, and friction. Speaks directly to the "I just want this done" mentality.

- **V1**: "No Photoshop. No studio. No VA briefings. No rejection emails. Upload your raw photos. Download marketplace-compliant images. That's the entire process."
- **V2**: "Centyr doesn't require design skills, marketplace knowledge, or technical configuration. You drag files in. You download compliant files out. The AI knows the specs — you don't have to."

---

### Angle 6: Speed-Based

Focus on the contrast between current painful speed and the new speed — makes velocity the central value.

- **V1**: "200 marketplace-ready product images in the time it takes to drink a coffee. Not 5 business days. Not 48-hour Fiverr turnaround. This session."
- **V2**: "Your competitors who launched on Zalando six months ago didn't have better products. They had faster images. Centyr processes your full catalog in minutes."

---

### Angle 7: Niche-Specific

Narrow the targeting to speak directly to the highest-value segment — Amazon-to-Zalando expansion — with language that only they would recognize.

- **V1**: "For EU Amazon FBA sellers with 50+ SKUs who are applying to (or getting rejected by) the Zalando Partner Programme: the only tool that converts your existing Amazon image set to Zalando's 2:3 portrait spec in one automated batch."
- **V2**: "If you're managing an Amazon.it or Amazon.de catalog and expanding to Zalando, you have two incompatible image format requirements. Centyr is built specifically to solve both — in a single upload."

---

### Angle 8: Anti-Angle

Challenge the assumption the buyer is currently operating under. Reframes the problem in a way that makes the existing "solution" look wrong.

- **V1**: "Your product photos aren't the problem. Every EU seller in your category faces the same Zalando rejection. The problem is that your image processor was built for Amazon — not for selling on multiple EU marketplaces simultaneously."
- **V2**: "Hiring a VA to manually resize your images for Zalando isn't a solution. It's the problem. Every inconsistency they introduce is another rejection. Automation isn't a shortcut — it's the only way to get consistent results at catalog scale."

---

## Top 3 Angles

**1. Angle 3: Pain-Based — "Zalando rejected your images because they want portrait format"**

Why it works: This is the highest-specificity, highest-urgency entry point in the entire customer journey. The Zalando rejection is a concrete event with a specific error message that every affected seller remembers. It names the problem so precisely that any seller who has experienced it will feel this was written for them. It also contains the implicit solution in the problem statement (portrait vs. square), which primes the reader to immediately understand why Centyr is relevant. This angle converts because it reaches buyers at the moment of maximum pain — which is when purchase decisions happen.

Best deployment: Top of funnel — cold ads, community posts, outreach messages. The first line of every touchpoint with the Zalando seller segment.

---

**2. Angle 7: Niche-Specific — "The only tool that converts your existing Amazon image set to Zalando's 2:3 portrait spec"**

Why it works: This is the credibility and differentiation angle. It speaks a specific technical language (Amazon.it, Amazon.de, Zalando Partner Programme, 2:3 portrait spec) that signals Centyr was built by someone who actually understands the EU marketplace landscape — not a generic tool that happens to have a Zalando preset. It also contains the competitive moat: "your existing Amazon image set" means no reshoot, no extra cost — which removes the most common objection before it's raised. For buyers who are already in the research phase and comparing tools, this angle closes the deal by making Centyr the only product that actually fits their situation.

Best deployment: Landing page headline for the Amazon-to-Zalando segment, product hunt listings, agency pitch decks, B2B outreach to seller communities.

---

**3. Angle 2: Time-Based — "Turn your entire product catalog into Zalando-compliant images in under 10 minutes"**

Why it works: Time is the resource EU e-commerce sellers with 50–500 SKUs are most acutely out of. The 10-minute claim is specific enough to be credible (not "instant"), dramatic enough to create a contrast with the current reality (5 business days, €4,000 studio), and immediately activates the "why am I not already using this?" response in a prospect who has been postponing the problem for weeks. The time anchor also reinforces the effort-reduction dimension — 10 minutes implies no setup, no learning curve, no briefing cycle.

Best deployment: Email subject lines, video ad hooks, landing page subheadline, trial activation screens.

---

## Recommended Use

- **Best for paid ads (cold traffic)**: Angle 3 (Pain-Based) as the hook — "Zalando rejected your images because they want portrait format." Lead with the rejection moment, then introduce Centyr as the specific fix. Pair with a free trial CTA ("process your first 25 free") to remove purchase friction entirely.

- **Best for landing page**: Angle 7 (Niche-Specific) as the headline — most credible, most differentiating, most specific. Supports conversion for traffic that is already problem-aware and comparison-shopping. Follow with Angle 2 (Time-Based) as the subheadline to reinforce speed. Include Angle 3 (Pain-Based) in the opening paragraph to confirm relevance for the Zalando rejection trigger.

- **Best for organic content (forums, community posts, LinkedIn)**: Angle 8 (Anti-Angle) — "Your product photos aren't the problem. Your image processor was built for Amazon, not for multi-marketplace EU selling." This format performs well as educational content because it reframes a belief the reader holds (blaming the photos) and positions Centyr as the insight, not as a product pitch. Educational reframes get shared and generate discussion, which drives community-native reach without paid amplification.

- **Best for high-ticket (agency or studio pitch, 5+ client seat accounts)**: Angle 5 (Effort-Based) combined with the ROI framing from the value stack — "No VA briefings, no Photoshop, no per-image studio cost. One tool that multiplies your throughput without multiplying your headcount." For the agency segment, the pain is margin destruction from labor hours, and the identity shift is from "manual retoucher" to "high-volume catalog production studio." The effort angle directly addresses that transformation.
