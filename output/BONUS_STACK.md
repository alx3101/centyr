## 6. Stack Strategy

**Why this order:**

Bonus 1 (Zalando Approval Accelerator) leads because the Zalando rejection event is the primary acquisition trigger. The majority of leads arrive at Centyr specifically because of a Zalando rejection. Leading with the bonus that directly names and resolves that event signals: "we were built for your exact situation." It also front-loads the highest perceived value bonus (€67) — the first item seen by the prospect is the highest-value item.

Bonus 2 (Glass & Jewelry AI Unlock) comes second because product-type skepticism is the highest-frequency technical objection and must be addressed before the prospect makes any decision. If they believe AI will fail on their product type, no other element of the offer matters until that belief is challenged.

Bonus 3 (Pre-Submission Safety Net) comes third to handle the residual outcome uncertainty that persists after the quality and format concerns have been addressed. This is the "what if it doesn't work despite everything" insurance layer.

Bonus 4 (Zero-Rejection Spec Vault) comes fourth as an independent verification resource. By this point the prospect is close to a decision; the Spec Vault gives them the means to verify Centyr's claims themselves rather than taking them on trust — which is more convincing than any additional claim could be.

Bonus 5 (Catalog Engine SOP Pack) comes fifth because the sustainability objection is primarily a conversion-from-trial objection, not a trial-entry objection. The prospect encounters this concern when deciding whether to subscribe after a successful trial, not when deciding whether to try the product.

Bonus 6 (10-Minute Catalog Kickstart Guide) is listed last but deployed first — it is the onboarding asset that appears on the signup confirmation screen and initiates the first trial session. Placement in the stack listing is strategic: it appears after the objections it resolves (friction, time) have been addressed in the prospect's mind, so by the time they see it they already understand what it eliminates.

**What objections remain unaddressed after the complete stack:**

The only objection the bonus stack does not directly handle is price — "I don't want to pay another monthly subscription." This objection is handled at the offer structure level by the free trial (no CC = no commitment), the ROI anchor (€6,000 studio alternative), and the conditional guarantee (reprocess for free if rejected). The bonus stack presupposes that these three mechanisms have already neutralized the price objection.
